Privacy Policy

A simple privacy notice for this static version of Happy Hunger.

Local browser storage

Favorites, recently viewed dishes and feedback may be saved in your browser using localStorage. This information is not sent to a Happy Hunger database in this static version.

Contact and order enquiries

Order and enquiry actions can open WhatsApp or your email application. Information you choose to send is then handled by those services according to their terms and privacy practices.

No payment processing

This version does not process online payments and does not include customer accounts or a backend database.

Future updates

If backend services, analytics, payments or customer accounts are added later, this policy should be updated before those features are launched.
